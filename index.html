Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can I use a fake or fun alias on Jobter while staying verified?

Yes! In the Alias Studio, you can pick any fun handle (like @LagosFlash or @AmalaPlug) so your public privacy is protected on campus and in your neighbourhood. Behind the scenes, every user completes a one-time NIN + Utility Bill KYC check so scammers cannot operate.

2. Is there a maximum budget limit for tasks or SafeLock™?

No. Task setters can set unconstrained minimum and maximum Naira budgets—from ₦1,500 campus errands to ₦5,000,000+ technical or logistics contracts. Likewise, your SafeLock™ vault can hold unlimited earnings.

3. Why can I only withdraw when my SafeLock™ reaches ₦20,000?

Completed task earnings move immediately from Escrow into your SafeLock™ Vault. To prevent micro-payout fraud and batch NIP bank settlement costs, direct bank withdrawals unlock as soon as your SafeLock™ balance reaches ₦20,000 or more.

4. How does the AI Nearby Radar protect my exact location?

Jobter never exposes your live GPS pin to strangers. Tasks display approximate neighbourhood landmarks and distances (e.g., 1.2 km from UNILAG Senate Building), and exact drop-off details are shared only inside the encrypted bilateral deal chat.
