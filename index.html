Need something done? (Task Setter)

Client Workflow
  1. Post your task with clear instructions, Nigerian state/landmark, required proof, and any budget range.
  2. Review offers from Jobbers and accept the price and profile that works for you.
  3. Approve submitted proof in the task chat to release payment.

Want to earn money? (Jobber)

Earner Workflow
  1. Browse or enable Nearby Radar to get alerted when tasks drop in your state, campus, or skill category.
  2. Make an offer within the Task Setter's minimum and maximum budget range.
  3. Complete the task & upload proof (photo or voice note) to credit your SafeLock™ balance.
