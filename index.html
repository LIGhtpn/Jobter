Help & Support Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Jobter escrow, SafeLock withdrawals, and security verification.

1. How does Jobter Escrow protect task setters and jobbers?

When a task setter posts a job or accepts an offer, the funds are locked in secure escrow. The jobber can perform the task with absolute peace of mind knowing the money is already deposited. Funds are only released to the jobber's SafeLock™ once the task is successfully completed and approved.

2. Why is there a ₦20,000 minimum threshold for SafeLock withdrawals?

SafeLock™ has no ceiling on how much it can hold. However, to maintain optimal bank transfer processing fees and ensure anti-fraud compliance under Nigerian financial regulations, direct bank withdrawals require a minimum balance of ₦20,000.

3. Is NIN and Utility Bill verification mandatory?

Yes. To protect our community from scammers and ensure all micro-task participants are genuine, claiming or posting tasks requires a quick NIN and utility bill verification.

4. Can task setters and jobbers communicate directly?

Absolutely! Once a task is claimed, both parties gain access to a real-time bilateral chat supporting text messages, photo/receipt uploads, and playable Voice Notes (VNs).
